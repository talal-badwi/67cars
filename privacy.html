Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

67Cars is made by Talal Badwi ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

We do not collect your data

67Cars has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run any server that receives or stores information about you.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your settings (sound, camera, graphics), your stats and best lap times.
  • The name you type in Settings (optional).
  • The picture you choose in Settings (optional). The app makes a small square copy of the one photo you pick and keeps it inside the app. It cannot see the rest of your photo library.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Playing with friends

Friends races use Apple's Game Center. While you are in a private room, your chosen name, your small picture (if you chose one) and your car's position are sent directly to the other players in that room so their screens can show you. They are not sent to us and are not kept after the room closes. Apple's handling of Game Center data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

67Cars does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children. Parents can turn off Game Center for a child in Screen Time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
