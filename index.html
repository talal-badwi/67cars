Common questions

  • How do I play with friends? Every player needs to be signed in to Game Center (iPhone Settings ▸ Game Center). One player taps PLAY WITH FRIENDS ▸ CREATE GAME and sends the 6-digit code; the others tap JOIN GAME and type the code. 2 to 5 players can race; empty places are filled by computer drivers.
  • The sound is too loud or too quiet. Use the sound bar in Settings or in the pause menu. All the way left turns the sound off.
  • The game feels slow or my battery runs down. Turn on Settings ▸ Graphics: Battery Saver.
  • How do I change my name or picture? Settings ▸ Name (up to 12 letters) and Settings ▸ Picture. Tap Picture again to go back to your car's photo.
  • My car left the track. It is put back on the road by itself after a moment, or tap RESET.
